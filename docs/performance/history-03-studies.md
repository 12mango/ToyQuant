# 3. The studies: what the L2 line asked and how it answered

**This page is retained outside the public tree** (`private/docs/performance/history-03-studies.md`). It was the part of
the performance history that carried the L2 line's measured answers: the queue model against real prints, the fee the
window would have needed, the acceptance number, the parameter sets the trend line tried, and the flatten band.

## What it recorded

Each chapter was one question, one measurement and the bar it was read against:

- **what the queue model decides** -- the same tape under four queue models, and why the fill count moves while the
  conclusion does not;
- **the maker fee each window would have needed** -- the capture a cell had to reach for a round trip to be paid;
- **the acceptance number** -- what a seat had to show before a size was argued from it;
- **the non-market-making signal** -- the parameter sets the trend line tried, and the family that closed it;
- **the flatten band** -- the close-out window and what it cost in capture.

## Where the method is public

[The L2 flags](../L2_FLAGS.md) documents every knob the studies swept, and [the tools](../TOOLS.md) names the sweeps and
the summary that turned their CSVs into the three numbers a cell is judged by. [The retrospective](../RETROSPECTIVE.md)
carries the *form* of a closure -- an input, a gate, a number -- without the numbers themselves.
